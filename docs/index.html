02 — How It Works

One dashboard. Five layers of filtering.

◈

Technical Screener

Full-market NSE + BSE scan using RSI, EMA crossovers, relative volume, and price action — pulled live, not curated.

⟡

News & Sentiment

Live headline scraping per symbol with heuristic positive/negative/neutral tagging, plus official NSE corporate announcements.

◇

Multi-Timeframe Confirm

Cross-checks daily signals against weekly and 4-hour structure before a setup counts as confirmed.

▦

ATR-Based Backtesting

Every rule is simulated bar-by-bar with real stop-loss and 2:1 target exits — not a fixed holding period fiction.

⌗

Position Sizing & Kite Tickets

Enforces a fixed risk budget per trade and generates ready-to-place Zerodha Kite order tickets — never places orders itself.

◉

Market Context

India VIX and live FII/DII flow data contextualize every signal against the broader market regime.

03 — The Evidence

Confidence is cheap.
Backtested honesty isn't.

Any chart can look convincing in hindsight. This system doesn't just flag a setup — it tells you, from historical simulation, how often that exact rule actually hits its target, hits its stop, or times out. If a rule doesn't hold up, the dashboard says so.

0:0 Minimum reward:risk enforced on every simulated exit
ATR Dynamic stop-loss sizing, not arbitrary fixed percentages
0% Max capital risked per trade, regardless of conviction
A confident-looking trend, wrapped in the analysis that decides if it's trustworthy.
